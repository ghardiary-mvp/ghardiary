PM case study on Urban Company's 90 day repeat booking · built from public posts only · not an Urban Company product

UC Discovery Engine

Why don't first-time Urban Company customers book again within 90 days? This engine reads what people write in public and tags every post against a fixed codebook, with checks on the AI.

How it works

~32,300public posts collected: Play Store reviews, Reddit threads, YouTube comments
~19,700kept after cleaning and removing duplicates and one-liners
1,020balanced sample tagged by Gemini, 14 fields each, every answer checked by code
77%agreement with 25 posts labelled by hand before any AI tagging
Every AI answer passes a gate before it is saved: values must be in the codebook, a post that is not relevant cannot carry a reason, and the evidence quote must appear word for word in the post or it is removed.

Try it: tag any review or post

Paste a real Urban Company review, a Reddit comment, or write one yourself. English, Hindi or Hinglish all work. It runs the same codebook and checks used on the 1,020 posts.
